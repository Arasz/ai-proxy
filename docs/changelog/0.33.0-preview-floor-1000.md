# 0.33.0: smaller previews from 1,000 tokens

Recoverable compression now previews tool result regions from 1,000 estimated tokens, not 3,000,
and sizes each preview to its block. The decision and the replay grid are in
[ADR 0015](../adr/0015-size-scaled-previews-read-and-diff-skip-and-a-replay-tool.md).

- The floor is 1,000 estimated tokens (was 3,000).
- The preview budget is the smaller of 1,500 and 0.4 times the block's original tokens.
- No preview is sent unless it is shorter than the reduced text.
- Numbered file reads and diffs under 6,000 reduced tokens are reduced but not previewed.
- `ai-proxy replay` has three new flags: `--budget-share`, `--minimum-saving` and `--skip-under`.
  Its defaults are the new values. See
  [Replaying stored requests](../how-to/replaying-stored-requests.md).
- Replay over stored traffic (snapshot 2026-11-21, window since 2026-11-06): preview saving
  rises from 6.18M to 16.30M estimated tokens gross. Worst-case net, after follow-ups at 0.90 per
  preview id, rises from +2.94M to +6.57M.

Each live session pays one prompt-cache break on its first turn after the upgrade, about 1.15 to
1.2 times the cached prefix (ADR 0005 F11). Regions between 1,000 and 3,000 tokens are now
previewed, and regions whose budget shrinks change bytes.
